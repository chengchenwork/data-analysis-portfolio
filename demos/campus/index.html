Method & data notes

How to read this dashboard

Room use (operational utilisation)

Room utilisation is max_headcount_hourly / Room Capacity. Observations above 150% are excluded from operational rankings, peaks, heatmaps, attendance and energy summaries, and retained in the outlier log.

Building metrics

Actual people and available seats are first summed across valid concurrent rooms in a building-hour. Average utilisation is the mean of those hourly rates; operational max is the highest rate.

Timetable fit, attendance and energy

Planned room fill is Planned Class Size ÷ Room Capacity; spare planned seats are Room Capacity − Planned Class Size. These are scheduled room-hour measures because the source has no stable class-session identifier. Attendance shortfall remains max(Planned Class Size − Actual Headcount, 0). A ghost scheduled room-hour has registered students above zero and actual / registered below 10%. Empty seat-hours are max(Room Capacity − Actual Headcount, 0) × 1 hour for scheduled lecture theatres.

Key dates and coverage

Both illustrative semesters contain 12 complete weeks. Adjusted mode excludes a fictional closure day in week 7; Observed restores it. The calendar panel includes all synthetic records, independent of the week filter. Timetable context describes planned size, not verified attendance.
